## Brands and important distinctions

- **CreativeDesk** is the planned rebrand of the existing MagicAI / Magical platform; these domains are not three independent products.
- **MailBoost** is the planned rebrand of **PowerInbox**, an internal PROCESS IN outreach tool.
- **Flowing** is an internal domain intelligence platform, not a publicly offered SaaS product.
- **PROCESS IN** is the company; **INHOST** ([inhost.mk](https://inhost.mk)) is its hosting and domains brand.
- **TeamDesk** is focused on sports training, coaching and athlete performance, not generic club CRM.
- “Product details pending confirmation” intentionally avoids inventing capabilities.
- Listing a domain does not grant an open-source license, publish proprietary code, or imply that the product accepts customers.
