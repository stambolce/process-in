## The product

WedFlow brings wedding planning into a shared workspace for couples and planners. The official experience connects budget, guests, seating, schedules and guest participation, so the plan and the day stay connected.

## How it works

| Step | Action |
|---|---|
| 1 | Add the date, venue and budget |
| 2 | Build the guest list and send invitations |
| 3 | Arrange seating and share the timeline |
| 4 | Use guest links for RSVPs, seating and photos |

## Core capabilities

| Area | Capabilities |
|---|---|
| **Budget** | Track allocations, categories and spending. |
| **Guest list** | Organize invitations, RSVPs and dietary details. |
| **Seating** | Arrange tables and guests visually. |
| **QR seat finder** | Let guests access their seating information. |
| **Timeline** | Share the day's schedule. |
| **Guest gallery** | Collect guest photos and videos. |
| **Vendors** | Keep quotes, deposits and contracts organized. |
| **Checklist** | Maintain the planning tasks. |

## Who it is for

Couples, wedding planners and collaborators coordinating a wedding.

## Practical scenario

A couple can organize invitations and seating while the planner maintains the timeline. Guests can contribute photos and access their seating information through the available guest experience.
