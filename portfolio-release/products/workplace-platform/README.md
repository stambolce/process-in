## A business task needs a specialist. A specialist needs a way to be found.

A company may need a landing page, a product video, translated content or a spreadsheet cleaned up. These are specific pieces of work, often outside the team's daily expertise. The first challenge is to turn “we need help” into a clear requirement, find someone who offers the relevant service and understand what that offer includes.

A freelancer faces the other side of the same problem: a skill needs more than a name in a directory. Potential buyers need to see the type of work offered, the professional behind it and whether it fits their task.

**[Workplace](https://workplace.mk/)** brings those two sides together. Developed by **[PROCESS IN](https://processin.mk)**, it is a marketplace where buyers explore professional services and freelancer profiles, while freelancers present services and discover projects.

Its central purpose is practical: **make a piece of work easier to find, describe and match with relevant expertise.**

## For companies and buyers: move from a need to a clear brief

Workplace is relevant when a business needs specialist help for a particular deliverable. It provides a place to explore offers across several disciplines without treating every requirement as a permanent hiring decision.

| Your situation | How to use Workplace | What to clarify before proceeding |
|---|---|---|
| You need design for a launch | Explore graphics and design services and review relevant profiles | Deliverables, formats, brand materials and deadline |
| You need a website or application task completed | Browse programming and technology offers | Existing system, precise scope and expected result |
| You need content adapted for another audience | Explore writing and translation services | Language, intended audience, tone and source material |
| You need spreadsheet or data work | Review data services and the provider's description | Input files, required output and handling instructions |
| You need creative work for a campaign | Explore video, audio, photography or marketing categories | Channels, asset requirements and the campaign brief |

Use the current listings to compare the available providers and the scope of their offers.

## For freelancers: present the service behind the skill

Workplace gives professionals a route to present their work through a profile and service offers. A buyer can move from a category to an offer and then to the person behind it.

A useful service presentation explains the result the buyer receives, the information needed to begin and the boundaries of the work. That makes a listing easier to assess than a broad statement such as “I do marketing” or “I build websites.”

The public platform includes an entry point for becoming a seller, freelancer discovery and project browsing. These support two complementary approaches: **be found through a service offer, and discover relevant work through projects.**

## Professional work across disciplines

Workplace's public categories cover **graphics and design, digital marketing, programming and technology, AI services, data, writing and translation, video and animation, music and audio, photography, business, finance, and personal development and hobbies**.

This breadth is useful when one initiative involves several kinds of expertise. A product launch, for example, may involve design, development and copywriting. Each piece can be researched in its relevant category rather than being forced into a single generic service.

## A practical approach to using Workplace

| Step | Buyer | Freelancer |
|---|---|---|
| **01 — Define the work** | State the outcome, inputs and deadline | Choose the expertise and deliverable you want to offer |
| **02 — Explore** | Search services, categories and profiles | Review the seller entry point and relevant projects |
| **03 — Evaluate the fit** | Read the offer and provider information | Compare the requested work with your actual scope |
| **04 — Prepare the details** | Clarify requirements before proceeding | Describe what is included and what you need from the buyer |
| **05 — Continue on the platform** | Follow the current service and account instructions | Follow the current provider or project instructions |

## Why use a marketplace for this work?

**A clearer starting point.** Begin with a service category or project requirement instead of an undefined search for “someone who can help.”

**A more informed comparison.** Read the offer and the provider's profile together. Compare the actual scope, not just the title or starting price.

**A place for focused professional work.** Buyers can explore expertise around a particular task. Freelancers can present a specific deliverable rather than relying only on a general professional label.

**A local point of discovery.** Workplace presents professional services to a Macedonian audience, with public language options for Macedonian, Albanian and English.
