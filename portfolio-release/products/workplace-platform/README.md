## The product

Workplace is a Macedonian marketplace for freelance services and projects. Buyers can explore service categories and freelancer profiles, while professionals can discover projects and present their skills.

## How to get started

| Step | Action |
|---|---|
| 01 | Choose a service or project route |
| 02 | Browse relevant categories |
| 03 | Review the offer or project |
| 04 | Register and follow the collaboration process |

## Core capabilities

| Area | What it offers |
|---|---|
| **Services** | Browse service offers. |
| **Projects** | Explore published projects. |
| **Freelancer discovery** | Find professionals. |
| **Categories** | Explore design, development, writing and AI. |
| **Saved services** | Use favorites for relevant offers. |
| **Buyer guidance** | Read platform and support information. |

## Who it is for

Freelancers and buyers seeking project-based professional services.

## Practical scenario

A business can compare a development or design offer; a freelancer can browse projects aligned with their skills.
