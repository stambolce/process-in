## The product

Shorty combines short links, bio pages and QR codes in a link management platform. Its official website also presents campaign organization, analytics and an AI copilot for link-related work. The domain currently uses ShortURL wording in parts of its interface; this showcase follows the current Shorty name confirmed in the portfolio registry.

## How it works

| Step | Action |
|---|---|
| 1 | Choose a link, bio page or QR workflow |
| 2 | Prepare the destination and branding |
| 3 | Share the resulting resource |
| 4 | Review clicks or scans in analytics |

## Core capabilities

| Area | Capabilities |
|---|---|
| **Short links** | Create recognizable, compact URLs. |
| **Bio pages** | Present important destinations together. |
| **Dynamic QR** | Update linked destinations without reprinting. |
| **Analytics** | Review clicks, location and device information. |
| **Campaigns** | Organize related links and QR resources. |
| **AI copilot** | Assistance with slugs, bio pages and insights. |
| **Link controls** | Password and expiry options are presented on the site. |
| **Team spaces** | Shared organization with role-based access. |

## Who it is for

Marketers, creators, agencies and organizations connecting offline materials with online destinations.

## Practical scenario

A venue can print a dynamic QR code for an information page, then update the destination as the campaign changes. A creator can collect essential links in one bio page.
