## The product

Glampoint is a salon booking and operations platform. Its public site presents appointment calendars, staff availability, service selection and reporting for salon businesses.

## How to get started

| Step | Action |
|---|---|
| 01 | Explore the salon platform |
| 02 | Review the available service and staff options |
| 03 | Organize appointments in the calendar |
| 04 | Use reports to review operations |

## Core capabilities

| Area | What it offers |
|---|---|
| **Appointments** | Schedule client visits. |
| **Calendar** | View the booking schedule. |
| **Staff scheduling** | Coordinate working hours and availability. |
| **Service selection** | Organize the services clients book. |
| **Reporting** | Review booking and revenue information. |
| **Locations** | The site describes branch management options. |

## Who it is for

Salon owners and teams coordinating client appointments.

## Practical scenario

Use a shared booking overview to coordinate a stylist's availability with requested services.
