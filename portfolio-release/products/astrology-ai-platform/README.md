## The problem and the solution

An astrology consultation involves a customer seeking a discussion, a practitioner delivering it and the account and payment context around that exchange. The experience needs to connect the customer's entry point with the consultation itself.

Astrology.mk is the PROCESS IN AI astrology and consultation platform. Its owner-confirmed scope includes a customer website, Android and iOS apps, astrologer management, paid real-time chat, wallet payments, spiritual services and a spiritual store. The product brings consultation and related service journeys under one platform.

## Who it helps

| Audience | Practical use |
|---|---|
| **Customers** | People looking to explore astrology consultations through web or mobile access. |
| **Astrologers** | Practitioners participating in the platform's consultation experience. |
| **Platform operators** | Teams managing astrologers, content and the consultation offering. |

## What the product brings together

### Customer website

A web entry point for exploring the platform and its consultation-related offering.

### Android & iOS experience

Mobile applications provide another customer channel for the platform.

### Astrologer management

The product includes management of the practitioners who provide consultations.

### Real-time paid chat

Customer and astrologer consultations include a paid real-time conversation flow.

### Wallet payments

The product's commercial scope includes wallet-based payments.

## Practical scenarios

### A consultation from a phone

A customer can use the mobile channel as the starting point for exploring the platform and following its consultation process.

### A practitioner-led conversation

The astrologer and customer meet through the real-time chat flow, with the consultation's payment context handled through the product's available account flow.

## Why consider this solution

The practical value is continuity between discovery and consultation. The website and mobile apps provide access; astrologer management gives the offering its practitioner context; paid chat and wallet payments connect that experience with its commercial flow. The spiritual service and store areas broaden the offering without requiring this presentation to invent individual services, prices or promised outcomes.
