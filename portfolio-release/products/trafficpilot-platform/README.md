## The product

TrafficPilot presents a service for configuring website traffic campaigns. Its public website describes destination URLs, geographic targeting, traffic-source choices and campaign reporting. This showcase describes those controls without promising search rankings, sales or independently verified traffic quality.

## Define the campaign before reviewing the visit count

A website operator evaluating a traffic service needs to understand its destination, targeting choices, source settings and reporting. A visit count alone does not explain whether the service fits the intended use.

TrafficPilot presents controls for configuring website traffic campaigns. The operator starts with a destination and reviews the available geography, source and campaign options. The public site also provides a demo route for evaluation.

### A practical way to assess the service

Define what you want to evaluate before starting a campaign. Specify the target page, examine the available settings and decide how you will review the resulting information. Keep campaign delivery metrics separate from the business outcome you ultimately care about.

This presentation describes the product's campaign controls. It makes no claim that purchased or generated traffic produces better search rankings, qualified customers or sales. The useful decision is whether the service and its stated terms match your particular purpose.
