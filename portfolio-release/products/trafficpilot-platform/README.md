## The product

TrafficPilot presents a service for configuring website traffic campaigns. Its public website describes destination URLs, geographic targeting, traffic-source choices and campaign reporting. This showcase describes those controls without promising search rankings, sales or independently verified traffic quality.

## How it works

| Step | Action |
|---|---|
| 1 | Review the service and its terms |
| 2 | Specify the destination |
| 3 | Choose available geography and source settings |
| 4 | Review the campaign and associated analytics |

## Core capabilities

| Area | Capabilities |
|---|---|
| **Destination** | Configure the campaign's target website. |
| **Geography** | Select geographic targeting options. |
| **Sources** | Review the available traffic-source settings. |
| **Campaign settings** | Configure the presented session options. |
| **Reporting** | Review delivery and campaign information. |
| **Demo route** | The site provides a demo registration route. |

## Who it is for

Website operators and marketing teams evaluating traffic campaign services.

## Practical scenario

An operator can review targeting and reporting options before deciding whether the service fits a campaign. Evaluate traffic quality and business outcomes separately from visit counts.
